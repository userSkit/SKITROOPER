# Intake — answer these before stage 1 is locked

Reply in chat or edit this file. Short answers are fine. Numbered so you can answer "1: 6h, 2: ..." in one message.

## Time
1. Hours per week you can give, honestly? (e.g. 5 / 10 / 20)
2. When are those hours? Evenings, weekends, daytime? (matters for tutoring vs. async work)
3. Can you reply to a customer message within ~2 hours during the day? (yes/no)

## Skills (rate each 0–3: 0 = none, 1 = basic, 2 = solid, 3 = could teach it)
4. Writing in English (CVs, emails, copy)
5. Excel / Google Sheets
6. Any Leaving Cert / Junior Cert subject you could tutor (name them + grade you got)
7. Any other language spoken fluently
8. Design (Canva-level counts)
9. Web basics (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify)
10. Video / audio editing
11. A trade, hobby, or past job with a skill people pay for (fixing bikes, photography, bookkeeping, coding, gaming, anything)
12. Your last 2 jobs or courses (one line each)

## Accounts & tools you already have (yes/no each)
13. Revolut or Irish bank account that can receive payments
14. PayPal (personal or business)
15. LinkedIn — and roughly how many connections
16. Adverts.ie / DoneDeal account
17. Fiverr / Upwork / PeoplePerHour seller account (any rating/reviews?)
18. Etsy / Gumroad
19. Instagram / TikTok / Facebook — any with followers, and roughly how many
20. Boards.ie / Reddit accounts (age of account matters for posting rights)
21. Canva (free is fine), Microsoft Office or Google Workspace
22. A phone that can take decent photos

## Constraints
23. Anything you will NOT do? (e.g. no video of your face, no phone calls, no tutoring kids)
24. Are you currently employed under PAYE? (only affects which tax form applies later; no action needed now)
25. Do you own anything sellable you don't need? (rough list, or "no")
26. Where in Ireland roughly? (Dublin vs. rural changes local-service options)

## Ambition check
27. If stage 1 works, can you scale hours up, or is the cap fixed?
