## Why personalised Irish gifts beats the gadget store
1. **Traffic exists without ads.** Etsy brings the buyers; a standalone gadget store brings none. Etsy fees are ~20–25% of price — https://www.printful.com/blog/how-much-does-etsy-take-per-sale — that is the price of the traffic and it is cheaper than ads.
2. **Q4 starts now.** Christmas gift searching on Etsy ramps from October. We are 8–12 weeks out. A late-starting shop can still catch it if listings are live by early October.
3. **The product is ours.** Nobody can undercut a design + personalisation combination the way they undercut a shower filter.
4. **Zero inventory, EU production.** Printful prints in Spain/Latvia and ships to Ireland in about a week; the supplier is the EU producer, so product-safety and customs issues fall away.
5. **The operator's own market.** Dublin operator, Irish audience, Irish diaspora buyers in the US/UK who pay in dollars and pounds.
