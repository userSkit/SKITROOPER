# Flipping — Dublin second-hand arbitrage with €150

## Why this beats ads at €150
- Adverts.ie: free to list in nearly all categories (https://www.adverts.ie/about). DoneDeal: Ireland's largest classifieds (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DoneDeal).
- Money cycle is days, not months. Every euro comes back with a margin or a lesson.
- Zero platform risk if you follow the rules: private-seller listings, no stock photos, no fake claims.

## Target categories (Dublin)
Priority order, chosen for demand, margin, and fitting on a bus/bike/car boot:
1. **Bikes** (adult hybrids/road, kids' bikes in Sept–Oct, turbo trainers in Oct–Nov). Buy €40–€120, sell €90–€220.
2. **Gaming**: PS4/PS5/Switch consoles and controllers, boxed games. Buy €60–€150, sell €100–€230. Test before paying.
3. **Prams, car seats, cots** (check recall lists; sell only complete, clean items). Buy €20–€60, sell €60–€140.
4. **Power tools** (Makita/DeWalt/Bosch). Buy €30–€80, sell €60–€150.
5. **Small furniture** (IKEA desks, chairs, shelving) listed "collection only, must go". Buy €0–€30, sell €40–€90.
6. **Phones** (iPhone 11–13, Samsung S-series). Higher risk: check IMEI, battery health. Buy €80–€150 only when the margin is ≥ €60.

Avoid: anything electrical that looks modified, designer goods (fakes), event tickets, anything the seller won't let you test.

## Buy rules
- Never pay more than 50% of the realistic resale price (checked against sold/current listings).
- Cash on collection in a public place, or Revolut on the spot after testing. No deposits, no couriers for buying.
- Pass on anything you cannot test or that has no photos of the actual item.

## Sell rules
- 6–10 photos in daylight, plain background, all faults shown.
- Title: brand + model + key spec + condition. Description: what it is, what works, what does not, why you are selling. No hype.
- Price 10% above target; accept the first offer within 10% of target.
- Relist every 7 days; drop 10% after 14.
- Ledger line for every buy and sell, receipts photographed.

## Weekly target
3–5 flips, €100–€200 profit. Reinvest all of it. €1,000 in 6–10 weeks if the pace holds; the 3-day reviews will say whether it does.

## What the local agent does
Scans public listings in the categories above for underpriced items (price ≤ 50% of comparable listings), records URL, title, asking price, comparable price, estimated profit, in `research/flip_candidates.md`. Never contacts sellers, never collects names or numbers.
