## The six candidates

### 1. CV + cover letter rewrite for Irish job seekers

- **What it is:** Take someone's existing CV, rewrite it to Irish-market norms (2 pages, no photo, profile paragraph, achievement bullets, ATS-safe formatting) and write one tailored cover letter. Claude does the drafting; you do a 15-minute quality pass and send.
- **Who sells it now and at what price (verified):**
  - Professional CV Ireland: from **€35** — https://www.professionalcv.ie/
  - CV Writing Services Ireland: from **€42** — https://www.cvwritingservices.ie/
  - CV Writing Service Ireland: from **€50** — https://www.cvwritingservice.ie/
  - YourCV.ie: **€59** headline, professional CV **€78**, cover letter **€32** — https://www.yourcv.ie/
  - The CV Centre: **€75–€425** — cited in https://www.findmyprofession.com/career-advice/resume-services-ireland/
  - Fiverr sellers: from **$30** for CV + cover letter + LinkedIn; reviews from **$15** — https://www.fiverr.com/expertresume/design-resume and https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/writing-and-copywriting/resume-service-cost
- **Why they're beatable:** The Irish agencies charge €35–€80 and quote 2–5 day turnarounds. We can do 24-hour turnaround at €25–€35 with unlimited one round of revisions, and sell person-to-person on Adverts.ie/LinkedIn where those agencies don't compete on warmth.
- **Capital:** €0 if sold via LinkedIn posts, WhatsApp, and free channels. €5 for an Adverts.ie Service ad (30 days) — https://help.adverts.ie/hc/en-us/articles/360001341745-Service-Ad-Fees
- **Time per sale:** ~30–45 min of your time (intake chat, quality pass, delivery). Claude does the writing.
- **Your 2-minute demand check:** Open r/irishjobs and r/ireland, search "CV" sorted by new. Count posts in the last 30 days asking for CV feedback or complaining about no responses. If ≥5, demand is real. Also open Adverts.ie → Services → search "CV" to see how many competing service ads exist and what they charge.
- **Honest risk:** Buyers are price-sensitive and many will ask a friend or use ChatGPT themselves. Conversion from enquiry to paid will likely be 20–40%. A bad CV that fails to get interviews will not usually cause a refund request, but promise only "a professional, ATS-friendly CV", never interviews or jobs.

### 2. Leaving Cert / Junior Cert grinds (online, 1:1)

- **What it is:** One-hour online grinds in a subject you scored well in.
- **Market price (verified):**
  - Typical Leaving Cert specialist grinds: **€30–€100/hr**; average one-hour grind **~€50** — https://www.superprof.ie/lessons/mathematics/ireland/leaving-certificate/ and https://aibf.ie/times/meet-the-company-providing-an-affordable-alternative-to-e50-grinds/
  - Superprof listings from **€15/hr** at the bottom end — https://www.superprof.ie/lessons/irish/dublin/
  - Grinds360 says 18,000+ students a year use its online platform — https://grinds360.ie/
- **Why beatable:** Parents pay €50/hr for teachers. A recent high-scorer at €25–€30/hr online, with Claude-prepared exam-focused notes and past-paper walkthroughs, undercuts and still has near-100% margin.
- **Capital:** €0. Superprof listing is free to create; Adverts.ie Services €5.
- **Time per sale:** 1 hour delivered per €25–€30. Prep partly done by Claude.
- **Your 2-minute demand check:** Search Adverts.ie Services and Superprof for your subject + county. Note the number of tutors and their rates. If dozens list at €20–€30, you need a differentiator (recent high grade, specific paper). Timing: it is September, grinds demand ramps now through May. Good timing.
- **Honest risk:** Hard cap on hours. €100 = 4 sessions, easy. €1,000 = 40 sessions, fine. €10,000 = 400 sessions at €25, which is 8 hrs/week for a year. Grinds alone cannot reach stage 4 unless you raise price or add group sessions. Good stage 1–2 engine, weak stage 3–4 engine. Also requires DBS-style trust: parents book from people who look real (LinkedIn, real name, results).

### 3. LinkedIn profile rewrite (bundled with #1)

- **Market price (verified):** The CV Centre charges **€50–€200** for LinkedIn profile writing in Ireland — https://www.cvcentre.ie/services/linkedin-profile-writing; also sold by professionalcv.ie, cvireland.com, procv.ie, cvwritingservice.ie.
- **Why beatable:** Same buyer as #1, near-zero extra delivery time. Sell as a €15–€20 add-on.
- **Verdict:** Not a standalone stage 1 play; it is the upsell for #1. Included in the offer.

### 4. Google Business Profile fix-up for Irish tradespeople and small local businesses

- **What it is:** Audit and fix a business's Google profile (categories, photos, description, review-request script, posting schedule). Sold to plumbers, electricians, salons, cafés.
- **Evidence of the gap:** Irish local-SEO guides state most tradespeople have claimed a profile but never add photos, posts or request reviews — https://legacygrowthpartners.ie/blog/local-seo/irish-tradesman-online-presence-2026/ and https://systemsetter.com/guides/local-seo-for-tradespeople-ireland. Older IEDR data: ~31% of Irish SMEs had no website in 2019 — https://www.irishtimes.com/business/work/lack-of-online-presence-means-irish-smes-losing-out-1.2602141 (dated; no 2025 figure found).
- **Price point:** No verified Irish per-job price found. Agencies sell monthly retainers.
- **Why beatable:** Agencies want €200+/month. A €60–€90 one-off "profile fix" is an easy yes for a sole trader.
- **Capital:** €0 (cold outreach by email/phone to businesses whose public profile is visibly weak).
- **Honest risk:** Slow. B2B cold outreach to tradespeople converts at low single digits and they are busy in September. Needs the operator to phone or email 50+ businesses to get 2–3 sales. Higher ticket, but time-to-first-cash is probably 2–3 weeks, not days. **Better as a stage 2 product** once we have €100 and a track record.

### 5. Spreadsheet / data cleanup gigs (Upwork, PeoplePerHour)

- **Market rate (verified):** Upwork median for Excel experts **$20/hr**, range **$12–$30** — https://www.upwork.com/hire/microsoft-excel-experts/cost/
- **Why beatable:** Claude can do most of it fast, so effective hourly is high.
- **Honest risk:** New accounts with zero reviews on Upwork struggle to win bids; Upwork charges "Connects" to bid, which eats the €10. PeoplePerHour similar. ### 6. Digital templates on Etsy / Gumroad

- **Fees (verified):** Etsy: **$0.20/listing**, 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 processing; some regions charge a **$15–$29 shop setup fee** — https://listify.studio/guides/etsy-listing-fees-2026-complete-guide and https://dodgeprint.com/blog/etsy-setup-fee-how-much-to-launch-a-shop-on-etsy. Gumroad: no monthly fee, **10% + $0.50** per direct sale, 30% via Discover — https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/gumroad-fees-2026
- **Honest risk:** Passive-income claims in this niche are mostly marketing from people selling courses (see the Gumroad results in my search). Real sellers report time-in-market is the main driver of sales. First sale often takes weeks and the Etsy setup fee could exceed the entire budget. **Killed for stage 1.** Could become a stage 3 product built from the CV/grinds materials we will already have (e.g. Irish CV template pack, Leaving Cert notes) — those would be products with proven demand.

## Skeptic's attack on the top 2

**Against CV rewrites:**
1. "Everyone can use ChatGPT now, why pay you?" — True, and it is why the price is €25–€35, not €78. The buyer is paying for someone else to do it, do it to Irish norms, and take responsibility for it looking right. Many job seekers are stressed and time-poor; that is the market. If your 2-minute check shows zero people asking for CV help in the last month, this objection wins and we go to grinds.
2. "Adverts.ie Services may be full of CV agencies." — Possible. The €5 ad is only placed if the check shows fewer than ~5 direct competitors at or below €35 in that category.
3. "Selling to friends/LinkedIn burns social capital." — Mitigated: the LinkedIn post is a plain offer, posted once, with a real price. No pestering. Outreach messages only go to people who publicly say they are job hunting ("Open to work" is public and opt-in).
4. "Refund risk." — Low if we never promise outcomes. Quality bar defined in `system/quality_bar.md`.
5. "Ceiling." — €35 × ~300 orders to reach €10k is unrealistic solo. This is a stage 1–2 engine that funds a higher-ticket stage 3 (business profiles, LinkedIn packages, template products). That is the plan, not a flaw.

**Against grinds:**
1. "You can only sell your own hours." — Correct; grinds are capped. Fine for €100 and €1,000, not for €10,000.
2. "Parents want qualified teachers." — Many do, but Superprof is full of students charging €15–€30 and getting booked. Your grade and recency are the credential.
3. "No-shows and cancellations." — Take payment before the session via Revolut link. Non-negotiable.
4. "It is September; will demand hold?" — Yes, grinds demand rises through to the mocks in Feb and exams in June.
