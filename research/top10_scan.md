## The three that actually fit

**#1 Social media management** is the highest score because it is the only one on the list with **recurring revenue**. €10,000 is 22 client-months at €450. Ten clients for two months is €9,000. Every other option on this list requires selling something new every single time. It is also almost entirely deliverable with AI leverage: I write the content calendar, the captions, the copy; the operator schedules and reports.

**#2 AI workflow automation** has the highest ceiling and the best story to sell right now, because demand is provably surging. The catch is it needs one reference client before anyone pays real money. Start it as an add-on to #1.

**#3 UGC video** is the fastest of the online options and needs zero audience, but the operator has to film. If that is a no, it drops off.

**Fastest cash of all is #4 or #5** — Airbnb turnover cleaning or gutter cleaning. Both can produce €100 within a week because it is autumn and the demand is seasonal and immediate. Both are physical work and neither compounds. They are the "bank €300 this week to fund the real thing" option, not the business.

## The honest problem, stated once

Six sessions in: five business models, €0 earned, and **not one account created yet** — no Stripe, no Marketplace listing, no Facebook business page, no social handles. Every model on this list, including the ones scoring 47, produces exactly €0 without those.

The bottleneck has never been the idea. Ogham prints would have made money by now if the listings existed. So would CVs. So would any of these. Whatever gets picked next, the test of whether it is working is **one account created and one offer sent within 48 hours**. If that does not happen, the next pivot will not fix it either.
