Our story

Slán Home is one person in Dublin, a laptop, and a stubborn opinion that an Irish gift should say something true.

Why names

Most of the Ogham stones that survive carry a single thing: a person's name. Someone chose to cut that name into stone so it would outlast them. Fifteen centuries later, we can still read them. That felt like a better starting point for a gift than a shamrock.

How it is made

Each print is set by hand for the name you give us. We check the meaning against standard references on Irish names, and if the sources disagree or say nothing, the print says so. You get a proof by email before anything is printed. The print itself is produced to order at a printing facility in the EU on 189 gsm matte paper, and posted tracked to your door. Nothing is held in stock, so nothing is wasted.

What we will not do

  • Invent a meaning for a name that does not have one.
  • Print before you have seen the proof.
  • Use fake reviews. When we have real ones, they will be here with the customer's permission.
