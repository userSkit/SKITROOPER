Questions

Before you buy

Which translation do you use?

Homer is Samuel Butler's prose, from 1898 and 1900, which reads at speed instead of fighting you. Every title's page names its source edition and its date. We use translations that are genuinely out of copyright, never a modern one reprinted without permission, and we say which is which.

Is this print on demand?

Yes, and we would rather tell you than have you work it out. Your copy is printed and bound after you order it, at a press inside the EU, and shipped straight to you. It is why there is no warehouse markup and no remainder bin, and why it takes three to five working days before anything is posted.

What does the book actually feel like?

A cased hardcover, roughly 14 by 21 centimetres, cloth-look board with gold stamped on the front and the spine, printed on cream uncoated stock. Not a hand-bound antiquarian book, and we do not claim it is. It is a well-made hardback you would be glad to be given.

Can I return it?

Fourteen days from the day it arrives, for any reason or none, under Irish and EU consumer law. Tell us and we refund the price in full. Details on the returns page.

Do you ship outside Ireland?

Yes. EU orders are printed inside the EU, so there is nothing to pay at the door. Outside the EU, duty may apply and is yours to pay. Delivery times and costs.

Who is behind this?

One person in Dublin who thinks the best books in the language deserve better than a supermarket paperback. Trading details are in the footer, as Irish consumer law requires. Say hello.
