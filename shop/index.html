Why Ogham

Ogham is the earliest written form of the Irish language. Around 400 stones survive, most of them in Kerry, Cork and Waterford, and nearly all of them say the same thing: a person's name. A name on stone was how you were remembered.

We think that is a better idea for a gift than most. Each print carries the name in Ogham, its meaning where one is recorded, and a plain-English line about the script, so the person receiving it knows what they are looking at. Read the story.

Questions people ask

Are the meanings accurate?

We use standard references for Irish name origins. Where a meaning is uncertain or disputed, the print says so. Where a name has no recorded meaning, we say that rather than invent one.

Can I return a personalised print?

Under Irish law the 14-day change-of-mind right does not apply to personalised goods, which is why you approve a proof before anything prints. If a print arrives damaged or differs from the proof, we reprint it free or refund you.

Do you ship outside Ireland?

Ireland only for launch. UK and US next; message us and we will tell you when.
