Worth knowing before you write

  • Order not arrived? Check the production time first: books are printed after ordering, which adds three to five working days before anything ships. The delivery page has the full timings.
  • Want to cancel? You have 14 days from delivery, no reason required. The returns page explains how.
  • Something damaged or wrong? Send a photograph and we will reprint or refund without asking for the book back.
  • Want a title we do not publish? Tell us. The list of out-of-copyright books is long and we are choosing the next ones now.

Checkout not working?

If a buy button says the checkout is opening shortly, that is because we are still finishing the payment setup. Email us the titles you want and we will take the order directly.
