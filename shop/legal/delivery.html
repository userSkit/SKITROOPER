Delivery

How long it takes, and what it costs

Every book is printed after you order it. That is the single biggest thing to understand about our timings: there is no shelf your copy is sitting on waiting to be picked.

The two parts of the wait

Production: three to five working days. Your book is printed, cased and bound at the press. This does not change with the delivery option you choose, because it happens before the parcel exists.

Transit: then the courier or postal service takes over, at the times below.

Where we ship and what it costs

Ireland2 to 4 working days after production. Delivery is included in the price.
European Union4 to 8 working days after production. Delivery is included in the price. Printed inside the EU, so there is nothing to pay at the door.
United Kingdom4 to 8 working days after production. Delivery charged at cost at checkout. Import VAT or duty may apply and is payable by you.
Rest of world7 to 14 working days after production. Delivery charged at cost at checkout. Duties and local taxes are payable by you.
Every orderShips as a single parcel, however many volumes are in it.

Tracking

When the press dispatches your parcel we email you the tracking number. If the tracking has not moved for five working days, write to us and we will chase it or reprint the order. You do not have to prove anything to us first.

If it arrives damaged

Photograph it and email us within a reasonable time of delivery. We reprint and resend at our cost, or refund you in full, whichever you prefer. We do not ask for the damaged copy back.

If it does not arrive at all

Under EU consumer law the risk stays with us until the goods are physically in your hands. If a parcel is lost in transit that is our problem to solve, not yours. Tell us and we will reprint or refund.
