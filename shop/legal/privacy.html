Privacy notice

What we do with your data

Short version: we take the minimum needed to print a book and post it to you, we share it only with the companies that take the payment and print the book, and we do not sell it or advertise to you with it.

Who the controller is

TRADER NAME, TRADING ADDRESS. Email: email.

What we collect, and why

Name and addressTo print the shipping label and get the book to you. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.
Email addressTo confirm the order and send tracking. Legal basis: performance of our contract.
Which titles you choseTo print the right books. Legal basis: performance of our contract.
Payment recordHeld by Stripe. We see that an order was paid, the amount, and the last four digits. We never see your full card number.
Order recordsKept for six years because Irish tax law requires it. Legal basis: legal obligation.

Who else touches it

  • Stripe processes the payment and holds the card data. We do not.
  • Our print supplier receives your name and delivery address in order to print the label and ship the parcel. It receives nothing else.
  • Our hosting provider serves this website and keeps standard server logs.

Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or share your details with advertisers, and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Cookies

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies and does not need a consent banner. If you go through to Stripe to pay, Stripe sets its own cookies necessary for the payment and fraud checks, governed by its own notice.

How long we keep it

Order and transaction records for six years, as Irish revenue law requires. Email correspondence for two years. Nothing else is retained.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we are not legally required to keep it, ask us to restrict how we use it, and ask for it in a portable format. Email us and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle it you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland at dataprotection.ie.

Transfers outside the EEA

Where a processor operates outside the EEA, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.
