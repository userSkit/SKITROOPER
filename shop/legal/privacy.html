What we collect

Your name, delivery address, email, the personalisation you give us (the name to print, dates), and your order details.

Why

To make and deliver your order, to send you the proof and tracking, and to meet our legal record-keeping duties.

Who sees it

Stripe processes payment; we never see your full card number. Our printing partner receives your name, address and the print file, only to produce and ship your order. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

How long

Order records are kept for six years as required by Revenue. Proof emails and print files are deleted 90 days after delivery.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us. You can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. Stripe's checkout sets the cookies it needs to process payment securely.
