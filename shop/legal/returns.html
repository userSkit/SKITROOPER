Returns and cancellation

Your 14-day right to cancel

Under Irish and EU consumer law you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive it. Email us at EMAIL with your order number, or use the form below. You then have 14 days to send the item back. We refund the full price, including our delivery charge, within 14 days of receiving the item or proof of postage.

Personalised items

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 the 14-day right to cancel does not apply to goods made to your specification or clearly personalised, such as name prints. That is why we email you a proof and print nothing until you approve it. Faulty, damaged or misprinted personalised items are always reprinted or refunded.

Condition

You can open and inspect the product as you would in a shop. If it has been used beyond that, we may reduce the refund to reflect the loss in value.

Faulty items

If anything arrives damaged or stops working within the first six months, tell us and we will repair, replace or refund at no cost to you.
