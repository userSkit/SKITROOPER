Returns and cancellation

Fourteen days, no reason needed

You have the right to cancel this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The period ends 14 days after the day on which you, or someone you name other than the carrier, physically receives the goods. If an order arrives in several parcels, the clock starts on the last one.

Why the right applies here, in full

Books printed to order sometimes get treated as bespoke goods, which are exempt from the right to cancel. That exemption covers goods made to your own specification or clearly personalised, such as a name printed on a cover. A standard title from our catalogue is neither, even though it is printed after you order it. So the full 14-day right applies to everything we sell, and we are not going to argue otherwise.

How to cancel

Tell us in a clear statement before the 14 days are up. Email the address in the footer, or post a letter to the trading address. You may use the form below, but you do not have to. Simply sending the books back without telling us is slower and riskier, so please write first.

What happens then

  • We refund everything you paid, including the standard delivery charge, within 14 days of getting the goods back or your proof of postage, whichever comes first.
  • We refund to the same method you paid with. There is no fee.
  • If you chose a more expensive delivery option than our standard one, we refund the standard cost rather than the upgrade.
  • Send the books back without undue delay and within 14 days of telling us.
  • You pay the cost of returning the goods. Please pack them well; you are responsible for any loss in value caused by handling beyond what is needed to see what the book is.

Faulty, damaged or wrong

This is separate from the 14-day right and is not limited to 14 days. If a book is misprinted, misbound, damaged in transit or simply not the title you ordered, we reprint it or refund you in full, and we pay the return postage. Your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 are unaffected by anything on this page.

Complaints

Write to us first and we will try to fix it. If we cannot resolve it between us, you can contact the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission in Ireland, or the European Consumer Centre Ireland for a cross-border purchase.
