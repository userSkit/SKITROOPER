Returns and cancellation

Your 14-day right to cancel

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 you can cancel an order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive it. Email with your order number, or use the form below. You then have 14 days to send the item back. We refund the full price, including any delivery charge, within 14 days of receiving the item or proof of postage.

Personalised items

The 14-day right to cancel does not apply to goods made to your specification or clearly personalised, such as name prints. That is why we email you a proof and print nothing until you approve it. Faulty, damaged or misprinted personalised items are always reprinted free or refunded.

Condition

You can open and inspect a product as you would in a shop. If it has been used beyond that, we may reduce the refund to reflect the loss in value.

Faulty items

If anything arrives damaged, or a fault appears within six months, tell us and we will repair, replace or refund at no cost to you. Your statutory rights are not affected.
