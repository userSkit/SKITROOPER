Questions

Can I check it before it prints?

Yes. You get a proof by email within 24 hours. Nothing prints until you reply "go".

Is the meaning accurate?

We use standard references for Irish name origins and say so on the print when a meaning is uncertain or disputed. If a name has no recorded meaning, we say that rather than invent one.

Can I return a personalised print?

Personalised items are exempt from the 14-day change-of-mind right under Irish law, which is why we send a proof first. Damaged or misprinted items are always reprinted free.
