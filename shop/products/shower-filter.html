Questions

Will it fix limescale on my shower screen?

It helps reduce it by filtering some of the minerals that cause scale. Existing scale still needs cleaning off once.

Does it lower water pressure?

Slightly on very low-pressure systems. On mains-fed and pumped showers most people notice no difference.

How often do I replace the cartridge?

Every 3–6 months for a household of two to four. Replacement cartridges will be listed here once the first batch ships.
