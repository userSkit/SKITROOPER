Questions

About sets

Can I mix any titles I like?

Any combination of the ten. Nothing is excluded and nothing costs extra, including the longer books.

Can I order two copies of the same title?

Yes. People buying gifts do this often. Just name it twice at checkout.

Do the sets arrive together?

Yes, in one parcel. It is why delivery is free on sets and charged at cost on single volumes.

Can I add to a set later?

You can order a single volume at any time and it will match, because the spine pattern does not change. That is the point of it.

Is there gift wrapping?

Not yet, honestly. The books ship in plain protective packaging from the press. If it is a gift, say so when you order and we will leave the price off everything.
