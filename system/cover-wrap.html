Cover wrap generator

Produces the back, spine and front of a case-wrap hardcover in one print-ready piece, using exactly the same artwork as the website. Set the numbers below, then print this page to PDF and upload the result to the printer.

Take the four measurements from the printer, not from here. Lulu and Bookvault both generate a template for your exact page count and paper stock, and the spine width in particular depends on the caliper of the paper you chose. The defaults above are a 146 × 216 mm board with a 21 mm spine, which is roughly a 330-page book on cream uncoated stock. Download the real template first, read the four numbers off it, type them in, and the artwork will fit it.
